History

From a Small Learning Center
to a Community Institution

Sanggar Kegiatan Belajar (SKB) Asmat was established as a non-formal education unit under the Asmat Education Office to serve learners beyond the formal school system. What began as a modest learning center has grown into a community institution offering equivalency programs, skills training, courses, and community learning activities.

Our journey reflects the community we serve — resilient, growing, and committed to lifelong learning.

"Every learner we support is a step forward for the whole community."
