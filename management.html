Meet the English Prime Team

The Course is managed by a dedicated team of qualified English Language teachers and staff committed to serving the Asmat community.

Maria Delfina Dhae, S.Pd., M.Hum
English Course Director

A cum laude postgraduate English Language Studies specialist with expertise in education, linguistics, and language teaching, empowering Asmat children to communicate confidently and meaningfully.

Responsibilities: Leads the strategic direction of the English Prime Course, manages curriculum design and academic quality, mentors instructors, coordinates with the Asmat Education Office, and ensures every class meets international CEFR standards.

Miss Indika Khoifin
English Instructor

An English educator with a strong foundation in education and linguistics, dedicated to clear, meaningful, and learner-centered instruction for Asmat children.

Responsibilities: Teaches Little Starter and Happy Beginner classes, designs daily lesson plans, guides students through communicative activities, and tracks learner progress through formative and summative assessments.

Miss Martha Clautilda Mindipko
English Instructor

An accomplished English educator with strong academic expertise in English language studies and advanced pronunciation skills.

Responsibilities: Focuses on pronunciation, intonation, and speaking fluency across all levels, leads English Day sessions and podcast activities, and provides individual coaching to help learners speak with confidence.

Miss Putri A. Fembes
English Instructor

An English Literature graduate with extensive teaching experience in English language institutions, skilled in clear, engaging, and accessible instruction.

Responsibilities: Leads reading and writing activities, guides students through stories and reflection reports, coordinates English Day events, and ensures learners build strong literacy foundations across all course levels.

Sir Todd Myers Nuh de Larson Fonataba
English Instructor

An English Education specialist with valuable experience as a tour guide and direct interaction with native English speakers, bringing authentic real-world communication into the classroom.

Responsibilities: Facilitates real-world conversation practice and native-speaker sessions, leads intercultural education activities, and mentors students in practical English for tourism, community life, and daily communication.
