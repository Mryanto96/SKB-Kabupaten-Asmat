Learning Programs at SKB Asmat

We offer a range of educational programs for children, youth, and adults in the Asmat community — from equivalency and skills training to English courses and community learning activities.

Education Programs

Non-Formal & Flexible Education

Life Skills Education

Equivalency Programs

Paket A, B, & C Equivalency Pathways

Non-formal equivalency programs that give learners the opportunity to complete formal education through flexible learning pathways.

Paket A — Equivalent to Primary School

For learners who wish to complete primary-level education through a non-formal pathway. Focused on foundational literacy, numeracy, and life skills.

Learn more

Paket B — Equivalent to Junior High School

For learners who wish to complete junior-high-level education through a non-formal pathway, with a focus on academic and life skills.

Learn more

Paket C — Equivalent to Senior High School

For learners who wish to complete senior-high-level education through a non-formal pathway, opening doors to further study and work.

Learn more
Note: Availability of Paket A, B, and C programs, along with specific requirements and schedules, must be confirmed with official SKB Asmat records.
