Welcome to SKB Asmat Sanggar Kegiatan Belajar (SKB) Asmat is committed to providing accessible, contextual, and learner-centered education for every member of our community. Through our English course, equivalency programs, skills training, and community programs, we aim to empower learners of all ages to grow personally, academically, and professionally. Our programs are built on a communicative and project-based approach, helping learners use real-life English, develop practical skills, and become lifelong learners. Together, let's learn, grow, and create a better future.

Featured Course

English Prime Course —
Speak Like a Pro

Our English Prime Course is built on the Communicative English Curriculum (CEC) — aligned with both the Indonesian Kurikulum Merdeka (KM) and the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). The course is learner-centered and contextual, focusing on real-life communication, speaking fluency, listening comprehension, practical vocabulary, and functional grammar.

  • Real-life communication & speaking fluency
  • Listening comprehension & pronunciation
  • Practical vocabulary & functional grammar
  • Reading for meaning & purposeful writing
  • Critical & creative thinking, CCU awareness

Course Levels

Core Pillars & Learning Path

A structured path from recognizing essential vocabulary to speaking English with confidence.

Little Starter

Essential vocabulary related to personal details, everyday life, and the surrounding environment.

Happy Beginner

Meaningful daily phrases and language expressions that learners can use right away.

Junior Talk (A1)

Personal details, activities, introducing yourself, the surrounding environment, and global culture.

Speak Like a Pro

Recognize, understand, and interact confidently about personal details and everyday life.

How We Teach

Our Methodology

A communicative, learner-centered framework that moves from modelling to independent production.

BKoF

Building Knowledge of the Field — building cultural and topic context before language work.

MoT

Modelling of the Text — being a model, showing learners how language works in real use.

JCoT

Joint Construction of the Text — guiding students in collaborative construction.

ICoT

Independent Construction of the Text — students independently produce spoken and written texts.

Teaching Methods used across the course:
Communicative Language Teaching · Project-Based Learning · Total Physical Response · Grammar Translation Method · Audio-Lingual Method · Genre-Based Pedagogy
