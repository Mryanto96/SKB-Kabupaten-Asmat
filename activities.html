Activities

Learning in Everyday Life

From classroom practice to real-world interaction — our activities help learners use what they have learned.

Podcast Sessions

Learners practice speaking, listening, and storytelling through podcast-style sessions that build confidence.

Native Speaker Sessions

Real interaction with native speakers that helps learners use English naturally and confidently.

English Day

A dedicated day for using English across the SKB Asmat community — games, talks, and interactive activities.

Team Up — Tasks & Projects

Group tasks and mini-projects that encourage collaboration, creativity, and communication.

Live Teaching

Guided live teaching sessions that model real language use and give learners direct feedback.

Intercultural Education

Activities that build cultural awareness and connect local identity with the wider world.
